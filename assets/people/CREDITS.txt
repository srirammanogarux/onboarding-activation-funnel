Recast 2026-09-04, second pass. The first worldwide set was environmental
photography — subjects mid-task, turned away from the lens, often small in a
busy frame. It read as a stock library rather than as people who had left a
review. This pass changes the brief: ONE person, FRONT-FACING, face large and
unobstructed, clean background, nothing in the frame competing with them. The
card was rebuilt to match — the face is now a 42px avatar and the quote is the
hero, with a store/network badge saying where the review came from.

Casting rules held: name, apparent gender and stated role/city all agree with
the photo, and every quote states an outcome its goal's learner actually wants.

Each slot ships two crops, both head-anchored, cut by tests/cast-crop.mjs from
the coordinates in tests/cast.json (fx/fy = head centre as a fraction of the
source; fw = head-and-shoulders box width as a fraction of source width). Use
tests/cast-measure.mjs to see the crop box drawn over the source before
re-cutting.
